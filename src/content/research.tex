\newcommand{\PhDEntry}{%
  \Role{Doctor of Philosophy, Computer Science}{University of Newcastle}{Synthetic bridging for domain adaptation}{Ongoing}
  \RoleContext{Developing synthetic bridging, a new approach to domain adaptation that helps machine-learning models transfer knowledge from historical data to newly deployed monitoring systems. It uses physics-informed conditional diffusion to reconstruct plausible histories from sparse observations, connecting older records with the richer data produced by modern sensors. Water networks provide the test case for a broader goal: allowing organisations to upgrade their monitoring while keeping years of historical patterns and events useful for learning.}
}
